How the points work

The program finds every magnet by its color, then rolls a pretend drop of water around the outside of the fold. The drop is about as wide as one paper-clip link. Any magnet the drop can touch counts as exposed; a hole inside the fold that the drop cannot get into does not count as water.

  • Right place (yellow or green hidden, any other color exposed): +1. Wrong place: −1. Partly exposed: in between.
  • A pink and a blue magnet within one link of each other make a salt bridge: +1 for each of the two.
  • Two pinks, or two blues, that close: −1 for each.
  • Two magnets that almost touch (closer than half a link): −1 for each.

One link is the length of a stretched magnet plus paper clip. The program measures it from the size of the magnets in the photo, so it does not matter how close you hold the phone.

Photo tips
  • Hold the phone flat, straight above the board.
  • Get the whole fold in the picture and let it fill most of the frame.
  • Keep markers, caps and other colored things out of the shot.
  • Tilt away from lamps and windows so the board has no shiny glare.
